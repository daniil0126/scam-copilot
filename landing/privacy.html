Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026
In short
  • Scam Copilot reads a PayPal page or email only when you press a button, and only on paypal.com.
  • What it reads is sent to its server, checked against rules, and the result is saved so you can see it in History.
  • It has no ads and no analytics, and it does not sell or share your data for marketing.
  • It cannot pay, cancel or edit an invoice.
  • It is a hackathon project tested on PayPal Sandbox. Do not use it with real invoices.

1. Who we are

Scam Copilot is a Chrome extension and a backend service that check PayPal invoices for signs of fraud. It was made for the PayPal AI Hackathon. The operator of the service is the Scam Copilot team. Contact: khan.daniil26@gmail.com.

2. What we collect, and when

DataWhenWhy
The visible text of the PayPal invoice page you have open (up to 20,000 characters) and its addressOnly when you press Check this PayPal invoiceTo read supplier, amount, dates, items and note, and check them

We do not collect your PayPal password, card or bank details, your browsing history, or the content of any page outside paypal.com. The content script cannot run on other sites.

3. Where it is stored

  • In your browser. Settings are kept in chrome.storage.sync, so Chrome may sync them across your devices. The optional secret for email checks is kept in chrome.storage.local and never syncs.
  • On our server. For each check we store the supplier name and email, invoice number, amount, currency, time, the findings and the explanation. For monitored invoices we also store each version of the invoice as PayPal returned it, alerts and monthly reports. For emails we store only the extracted fields, not the HTML. A record of actions (for example report created) is kept for audit.

4. Who else receives data

  • PayPal. The backend reads invoices through PayPal’s Invoicing API with read-only calls. It does not pay, cancel or change anything.
  • An AI model provider (optional, off unless the operator turns it on). When the AI review or the chat is on, a reduced version of the invoice is sent to OpenRouter, using free models only: supplier name, amount, dates, the findings and the note. Before sending, email addresses keep only their first letter and domain, phone numbers are replaced, and query strings of links are removed. Invoice text is sent as quoted data, never as instructions. Free models can be rate limited, and their providers may use prompts for training, so do not use real invoices with it.
  • Hosting. The server runs at Render, which processes the data on our behalf.

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising or credit decisions, and do not share it with anyone else except as the law requires.

5. How the data is used

Only to run the checks you ask for, show them to you, send in-app alerts and build your monthly report. The language model explains a result and answers questions. It does not set the score, which comes from fixed rules. We use the data only for these features and for keeping the service secure.

7. Security

  • Data travels to the server over HTTPS. The server accepts requests only from the extension.
  • Secrets such as API keys and tokens are kept out of logs. The server is not given PayPal live credentials.
  • The chat can read two things from PayPal, the invoice list and a single invoice, and nothing else.

No system is perfectly secure. Treat this as a prototype.

8. Keeping and deleting data

Checks and monitored invoices stay on the server until we delete them. To have your data deleted, write to khan.daniil26@gmail.com with the invoice number or the address you used, and we will delete it. Removing the extension deletes its settings from your browser.

9. Chrome Web Store limited use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use the data only to provide the single purpose of the extension, checking PayPal invoices, and we do not transfer it to others, or use it for ads, or let people read it, except to provide the feature, for security, to comply with law, or with your consent.

10. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and describe the change in the extension’s release notes.
